Step & Steel

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

The short version: your workouts, runs and body details stay on your phone. Step & Steel has no user accounts and no servers that store your training information, and we don't use advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking. A few service providers handle routine technical information, such as your IP address, so we can deliver videos and this website. This policy explains exactly what, why and for how long.

Who we are

Step & Steel is a fitness app and website operated by Bradley Gibbons, a sole proprietor in Alberta, Canada ("Step & Steel", "we", "us"). We handle personal information in line with Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and other Canadian privacy laws that apply to us.

Information that stays on your phone

To build your plan and track progress, the app saves what you enter, including:

  • Your training choices, such as experience level and goals for running and weights
  • Your weekly workouts and runs, including any you build in My Workout
  • Sessions you mark as done, and the runs, walks and rest days you log, with distance and time
  • Nutrition details: bodyweight, activity level and dietary goal
  • App settings, such as reminder time, video volume, your chosen coach and your acceptance of our safety notice
  • Your free trial start date and subscription status

This information is stored only on your device. The app does not send it to us or to anyone else. On iPhone, the app's saved information is excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

Information handled by service providers

Some features need to connect to the internet. When they do, a service provider receives the technical information that any internet connection involves. This is ordinary server activity, not advertising tracking, and none of it includes your workouts or body details.

Workout videos (bunny.net)

The full-length follow-along videos are streamed from our video delivery provider, BunnyWay d.o.o. (bunny.net). To send you a video, bunny.net's servers receive your device's IP address, the name of the video file requested, and standard request details such as your device's software version (its "user agent") and the time. bunny.net uses this only to deliver the video and protect its network. Its request logs are kept for 3 days, and by default it records IP addresses in anonymized form. Videos you have watched are saved in your phone's temporary storage so they play faster next time; your phone can clear this storage when it needs space. The short demo clips in the exercise library are built into the app and don't use the internet.

This website (GitHub Pages and Google Fonts)

stepandsteel.com has no forms, sign-ups, cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted by GitHub, which logs the IP address of website visitors for security purposes. Our pages also load a typeface from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser information when it delivers the font. Links in the app to our Terms of Use and this policy open this website.

Emails to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, name (if included) and whatever you choose to write. Our email is provided by Zoho and stored in its Canadian data centre. We use it only to reply to you and keep a record of the conversation.

Reminders

Daily workout reminders are scheduled on your phone by your phone's operating system. We don't use push notification servers, and reminder details are not sent to us.

Apple and Google

You download Step & Steel from the Apple App Store or Google Play, and they handle your account with them under their own privacy policies. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may send us crash reports and summary usage statistics that do not identify you. If you join our beta test through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares your name, email address, device details, crash reports and any feedback you send with us so we can run the test.

Subscriptions

Your free trial and subscription status are currently tracked on your phone, and the app does not yet take payments. When paid subscriptions launch, you will buy and manage them through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process the payment, and we never receive your card or banking details. Before that happens, we will update this policy to describe exactly what subscription information we receive.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking tools, and we don't build profiles of you.
  • We don't ask for or access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health app data.

Information outside Canada

Some of our service providers handle information outside Canada. bunny.net is based in the European Union and delivers videos from servers around the world, including the United States. GitHub and Google are based in the United States. Information they handle is subject to the laws of those countries, and may be accessed by courts or authorities there. If you have questions about how these providers handle information, contact our Privacy Officer below.

How long information is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • bunny.net video logs: 3 days.
  • GitHub and Google: according to their own policies, linked above.
  • Support emails: only as long as we need them to help you and keep reasonable business records, after which we delete them.

Deleting your information

You can edit or delete logged activity in the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes the information it stored on your phone. A few copies may remain elsewhere for a time: on Android, Google may have included the app's information in your device backup, which you can manage in your Google account; service provider logs expire as described above; and your past emails with us stay in our mailbox until we delete them. Apple and Google also keep their own records of your downloads and any purchases.

Your rights: access, correction and complaints

You can ask us what personal information we have about you, how it has been used and who it has been shared with, and ask us to correct anything that is wrong. Because your training information stays on your phone, we usually hold very little: typically only emails you have sent us. Contact our Privacy Officer below. We will respond within 45 days, as Alberta's PIPA requires, and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

Children

Step & Steel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app or website handles information, for example when paid subscriptions launch, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Privacy Officer

Bradley Gibbons is responsible for Step & Steel's privacy practices. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email our Privacy Officer at support@stepandsteel.com.
