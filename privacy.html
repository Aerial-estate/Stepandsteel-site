Step & Steel

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version: the Step & Steel app keeps your training information on your phone. We don't run accounts, we don't collect your workouts or health details on our servers, and we don't use ads, analytics or tracking.

Information you enter

To build your plan and track progress, the app stores what you enter, including:

  • Your training choices, such as experience level and goals for running and weights
  • Your weekly workouts and runs, including any you build yourself in My Workout
  • Sessions you mark as done, and runs, walks and rest days you log, including distance and time
  • Nutrition basics: bodyweight, activity level and dietary goal
  • App settings, such as reminder time, video volume and your chosen coach

All of this is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Workout videos

Follow-along videos are streamed from our video delivery provider, bunny.net. Like any website or online service, it receives standard technical information needed to deliver the video, such as your IP address and the time of the request. This is not linked to you or your training data. Videos you have watched are saved on your phone so they play faster next time.

Reminders

If you turn on daily workout reminders, they are scheduled on your phone by your phone's operating system. We do not use push notification servers, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google handle the payment, and we never see your card details. We only receive the information needed to confirm your subscription status. Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't use analytics that follow you across apps or websites.

Crash reports and beta testing

If you choose to share crash reports or diagnostics with Apple or Google, or send feedback through TestFlight, they may share that information with us to help fix problems, under their own privacy policies.

Children

Step & Steel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You control the information in the app. You can edit or delete logged activity in the app, and deleting the app removes everything it stored. If you have questions about your information, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@stepandsteel.com.
